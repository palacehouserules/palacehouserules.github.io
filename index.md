# Palace: House Rules

**Shed card game: bots & friends.**

The card game you learned in a hostel, on a ferry or round a kitchen table, also known as Shed or Karma. Play it
against bots with real personalities, or online with friends using a 5-letter room code. No accounts, no ads.

- Up to five bots, from hopeless Ben to the card-counting Professor
- Online rooms for 2 to 6 players
- Classic rules, or your own house rules
- A 2-minute tutorial for new players
- Plays offline, perfect for flights and buses
