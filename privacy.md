# Palace privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Palace is a card game. You can play against bots on your phone, or online with friends who join your room with a
short code. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. This page explains what that means for your information.

**Who we are:** Palace is made by an independent developer in the United Kingdom, named as the seller on Palace's App
Store page. We are responsible for the information described here. Contact:
[{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}).

## Playing on your own

Games against bots stay on your phone. Your settings, saved game, chosen bots and statistics are stored only on the
device, and nothing about them is sent to us.

## Playing online

To run an online game, the app sends the following to the Palace game server:

- **The name you type.** Everyone in your room sees it. Names are checked against a list of offensive words.
- **Your moves** in the game.
- **A random player ID** made by the app when you first play online. It identifies nobody outside the app. The
  server shows other players only a scrambled version of it, which is used so that blocking, muting and reporting
  work.
- **The list of players you have blocked** (as those scrambled IDs), so you aren't put in a room with them.

The server also gives your phone a random seat token so that you can get back into your seat if the connection drops.

**Reports:** if you report a player, the server records the room code, the name they used, the reason you chose and
the time, so we can look into it. Reports are kept for up to 90 days, then deleted.

**How long it's kept:** a room, with its names, moves and tokens, is deleted when everyone leaves, about 10 minutes
after nobody is connected, or after 2 hours without activity, whichever comes first. The server keeps no record of
your games after that. It doesn't log your IP address; it holds it in memory for one minute only, to limit how often
a phone can try room codes. To check the game is fast enough around the world, the server counts, each day, how many
players connect through each of our host's regions and how quick their connection is, without storing who they are.

**Where it's kept:** the game server runs in London, UK, on Fly.io, our hosting provider. Fly.io may keep standard
connection logs for a short time to run and protect its network.

**On your phone:** your player ID, the name you last used, your block and mute lists and the room you're in are stored
on the device so that the app can get you back into a game. Deleting the app deletes them.

**Who sees what:** other players in your room see your name, the cards you play (as at a real table) and whether
you're connected. They never see your hand or your face-down cards; those stay on the server.

## Why we use it (UK GDPR)

We use the information above only to run the online game you've asked to play and to keep it fair and safe (blocking,
muting, reporting). Under UK data protection law, our basis is our legitimate interest in providing the game you chose
to play.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of any information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. In practice, rooms
are deleted automatically within hours, reports within 90 days, and everything else is on your own phone, where
deleting the app removes it. Email us with any request and we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office
at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).

## Children

Palace is not made for children and isn't aimed at them. The name you type for online games should not be your full real name.

## Contact

To ask about this policy, report a player or a problem, or ask for anything to be deleted, email
[{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}). You can also use **Settings → Report a problem** in the
app.
