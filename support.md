# Palace support

**Email:** [{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}) (or **Settings → Report a problem** in the app). We reply within two working days.

## Reporting a player

If someone in an online room uses an offensive name or behaves badly, tap their name in the room (or on the results):

- **Report** sends us the room code, their name and the reason you pick. We look at every report within two working
  days, and a player reported by several people is removed from the room.
- **Mute** hides their name from you (they show as "Player 3"). Undo it any time in **Settings → Muted players**.
- **Block** means you won't be put in a room with them again. Undo it any time in **Settings → Blocked players**.

You can also email the address above with the room code, the name they used and roughly when it happened.

## Playing online

- **Create a room**, then tap **Share** to send the code to your friends. They tap **Play online → Join** and type it.
- **2 to 6 players.** The host can add bots to fill seats, and choose the rules and turn timer.
- **If your connection drops** or you switch apps, you have 30 seconds to come back before a bot plays your turns
  for you. Open the app again and it puts you straight back in your seat.

## Playing against bots

- **Choose your bots** on the Play screen: add up to five, from three characters at each level: Easy (Ben, Mr Tranquilo,
  Definitely Not Ben), Medium (Ellie, The Student, Penelope) or Hard (The Professor, Dr Kim, The Dean).
- **Change the rules** with **Change Rules**: Classic, Simple, or your own house rules. **What are the rules?**
  explains whichever rules you've chosen.
- Your game is saved as you play, so you can close the app and carry on later.

## New to the game?

**Settings → How to play** has a 2-minute tutorial against Ben, and **Rules in 30 seconds**.

## Something isn't working?

Email us and tell us what happened, what you expected, your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General →
About). A screenshot helps.
